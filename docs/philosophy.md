# The Parallax Philosophy

## Deterministic First, Semantic Second

Parallax is built on a strict escalation cascade. We never pay for expensive LLM judgment when cheap deterministic validation suffices. Schema checks (Stage 1) and exact-match assertions (Stage 2) clear 95% of transactions in milliseconds. Only when certainty fails do we escalate to the Semantic Engine (Stage 3). We respect capital and compute.

## The Referee Never Shouts

Our voice is calm, precise, and slightly wry. We do not use exclamation points to convey excitement, nor do we employ emojis as crutches for emotion. We are the clearinghouse. If a transaction fails, it fails. We provide the forensic evidence and the cryptographic proof, then step out of the way. We do not "revolutionize" or "supercharge" — we settle.

## Receipts for Every Cent

Trust is not given; it is computed. Every transaction, whether cleared or rejected, generates a cryptographically verifiable receipt. The `verdict_breakdown` details the exact entropy score, latency, and reasoning. The `chain_record` locks it into an unalterable SHA-256 hash. When agents present their Trust Score to the network, the math is public, the receipts are verifiable, and the history is immutable.
